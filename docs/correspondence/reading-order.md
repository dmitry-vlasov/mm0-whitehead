### `Theory/Search.lean` (54 declarations, 30 listed)

The reference engine (`docs/THEORY.md`, "The reference engine"): the proof variant tree and the substitution proof trees of the draft's Definitions 4.1 and 4.2, computed round by round, breadth first, every entry kept. Pure, and it runs; written as folds and recursions, so that the invariants (`Spt.lean`) can be proved about it. One departure from the draft's wording of Definition 4.2: the entry of an assertion node joins the children's answers *with the node's own unifier* `θ_a`; without it the metavariables of the parent that `θ_a` binds would be lost from the answer. The 2017 paper's correctness proof has the witness `θ_0 ∘ η`, and Russell composes `prop.sub` with the join (`ruAddProof`).

### `Theory/Generality.lean` (53 declarations, 13 listed)

Generality (the 2017 paper's Theorem 2, the draft's 4.5): the proof of an entry at the root is more general than every proof of an instance of the statement congruent to it. The argument. A proof `π` congruent to the entry's tree `T` gives a substitution `ρ` on the whole state: the instance's substitution on the statement's variables, and at the renamed binders of every assertion node of `T` what `π`'s step there gives them. This is well defined because the nodes of `T` are distinct (their numbering keeps their variables apart, and a tree never visits a node twice: `anodes_nodup`). Then `ρ` solves the equation of every node of `T` and every leaf (`sim`), so it absorbs the tree, so it is an instance of the entry's answer, which is most general among what absorbs the tree (`entry_mostGeneral`); and `π` is the proof of `T` under `ρ`.

### `Theory/Completeness.lean` (68 declarations, 20 listed)

Completeness (the 2017 paper's Theorem 3, the draft's 4.6): every proof of an instance of the statement is, after enough rounds, an instance of the proof of an entry at the root congruent to it. The first part, here: what the rounds do. A state grows (`Le`): nodes are kept, marks are kept, attempts are kept. After a round exactly the nodes that existed before it are expanded (`expandAll_expanded_iff`); a node that exists after round `m` has tried every assertion by round `m + 1` (`tried_of_lt`); and an assertion node was made in the round after its parent appeared, so its children are bounded by that round's size (`anode_round`).
